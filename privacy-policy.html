Privacy Policy

What's In My Fridge? (FridgeChef) · Last updated: 30 October 2026

This policy explains what the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and what you can do about it. It covers the Android app "What's In My Fridge?" (also called FridgeChef), published by Greg Rycroft ("we", "us").

The short version. Your pantry, recipes, shopping list, meal plan and nutrition log are kept on your phone. You only get an account if you choose to make one; then your saved recipes, pantry, shopping list and meal plan (but not your own recipe photos, and not your nutrition log, which stays on the phone) are also stored in your account so they match across your phones. Some features send text or a photo to our server and on to Google's Gemini AI to work: the fridge photo scan, the AI recipe fallback, and Chef chat. These are listed below. Analytics is off unless you turn it on. You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app.

1. What the app collects

What you put into the app

  • Kitchen content — ingredients you type or scan, saved recipes (with your notes, tags and ratings), pantry items and use-by dates, shopping list, meal plan, and the meals you log in the nutrition tracker. These are kept in encrypted storage on your phone.
  • Preferences — your dietary choices, allergens, nutrition goals, kitchen equipment, region and units.
  • Your own recipe photos — stay on your phone. They are never uploaded, never synced to your account and not included in backups.
  • Account details (optional) — if you create an account or sign in with Google or Apple: your email address and display name. We never see your password.
  • Backups you make — a text file of your pantry, recipes, shopping list and meal plan that you create and keep yourself (it does not include your nutrition log or photos). We do not receive it.

What leaves your phone, and when

FeatureWhat is sent, and to whom
Fridge photo scanThe photo is sent over an encrypted connection to our recipe server, which passes it to Google's Gemini API to list the ingredients in it. It is used only to produce that list. We do not store it after the request, we do not link it to your account, and we do not use it to train any model. (You are asked to agree the first time.)
AI recipesWhere your phone supports Android's on-device AI, your ingredients stay on the phone. Otherwise your ingredients, diet, allergens, equipment, region and units are sent to our server and on to Google Gemini to write the recipe. If neither works, the app writes a basic recipe on the phone with its own built-in engine.
Chef chatYour message, the recipe you are looking at, and your diet and allergens are processed on the phone where it supports on-device AI, and otherwise sent to our server and on to Google Gemini.
Importing a recipe from a web pageThe page address you give is fetched directly from your phone by the app. The website you ask for can see your internet address, as with any browser. Nothing is sent to us.
Barcode scanThe barcode is read on your phone. The barcode number is then looked up on Open Food Facts (a public food database), which can see the number and your internet address. No picture is sent.
Reporting a problem with AI contentIf you tap "Report a problem", the recipe or chat text you are reporting, the reason you give and the app version are sent to our server and stored so we can review them. They are not linked to your account.
Cloud sync (only if you sign in)Your saved recipes, pantry items, shopping list and meal plan are stored in your account in Google Firebase (Firestore) so they match on your other phones, including a note of anything you deleted so the deletion reaches your other phones.
AdsGoogle AdMob uses your device's advertising ID to show ads, unless you have bought the ad-free upgrade. In the UK and EEA you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
Crash reports and settingsFirebase Crashlytics receives technical error details and device information so we can fix faults. Firebase Remote Config fetches the app's settings. Neither includes your recipes or pantry.
Usage statistics (off by default)Only if you switch on "Share usage statistics" (in onboarding or Settings): which screens you open, which features you use and your diet and kitchen choices, sent to Firebase Analytics against a random app ID, not your name. You can switch it off again at any time.
PurchasesThe one-off "Remove ads" purchase is handled by Google Play. We never receive your card details; the app asks Google Play whether you own it. To confirm a purchase is genuine, the app sends the purchase receipt code to our server, which checks it with Google Play and keeps nothing: it is not stored or logged.

What our server records

Our recipe server runs on Google Cloud. To stop misuse and control cost it uses your internet address to count how many requests one connection makes a day. Those daily counters store only a one-way scrambled form of the address, not the address itself. Google Cloud's ordinary server logs may also hold the address for a short time. The server also checks Firebase App Check (Google Play Integrity), which confirms a request comes from the genuine app on a real device.

2. Why we use your information

  • To write recipes and run the features you ask for.
  • To keep your kitchen matching across your phones, if you sign in.
  • To keep the app working: diagnosing crashes, preventing misuse and controlling cost.
  • To review reports of unsafe or inappropriate AI content.
  • To show ads, which fund the free version.
  • To understand which features are used, only where you have allowed it.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your recipes or photographs to train our own models. AI recipes and chat replies can be wrong: always check ingredients for allergens yourself and cook food safely. FridgeChef cannot guarantee that any recipe is free of an allergen.

4. Who we share with

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Analytics if you allow it, Crashlytics, Remote Config, App Check) — accounts, synced content, diagnostics.
  • Google Cloud — hosts our recipe server.
  • Google Gemini API — when a fridge photo, a recipe request or a chat message is processed by our server.
  • Google AdMob — advertising.
  • Google Play — the ad-free purchase.
  • Open Food Facts — barcode look-ups.

We may also disclose information where we are legally required to.

5. International transfers

Our providers may process data outside the UK and EEA, including in the United States, relying on safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. How long we keep it

  • On-device data — until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Synced account data — until you delete your account. A note of a deletion is kept for up to 120 days so it can reach your other phones.
  • Reports of AI content — up to 12 months.
  • Daily request counters — a few days.
  • Crash and analytics data — for the retention periods Firebase applies, up to 14 months.

7. Your rights and choices

  • Delete your account and all synced data — in the app, Account → Delete account (you confirm it is you first). See our account deletion page, including how to ask for deletion without the app. The pantry, recipes and lists on your phone stay on your phone until you delete them or uninstall.
  • Switch usage statistics on or off — Settings → Privacy & Data Insights → Share usage statistics.
  • Change your ad choices — Settings → Ad privacy choices (UK and EEA), or reset your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Remove ads — the one-off ad-free purchase in Settings.
  • Get your data — Settings → Back up and restore creates a file of your pantry, recipes, shopping list and meal plan.

If you are in the UK or EEA you also have the right to access, correct, export, or restrict the processing of your personal data, and to object to it. To exercise any of these, email us at gregmish69@gmail.com or greg@gregrycroft.co.uk. You may also complain to your local supervisory authority; in the UK that is the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Account sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication and your password is never stored by the app. Your kitchen content and preferences are held in encrypted storage on your device. Synced data is protected by security rules so that only your signed-in account can read or change it. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top, and for significant changes we will tell you in the app.
