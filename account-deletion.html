Delete your FridgeChef account

FridgeChef: AI Recipe Planner · Greg Rycroft

You can delete your FridgeChef account and everything synced to it at any time. There are two ways to do it.

Option 1 — From inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open FridgeChef and go to Account.
  2. Scroll to Delete account.
  3. Confirm when prompted. If you signed in a while ago you may be asked to sign in again first — this is a security check to confirm it is really you.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Option 2 — By email

If you no longer have the app installed, email gregmish69@gmail.com from the address registered to your account, with the subject "Delete my account".

We will confirm your identity, delete the account, and reply to confirm within 30 days — usually much sooner.

Data on your phone. Deleting your account removes what is stored on our servers. Recipes, pantry items and settings saved locally on your device are removed when you uninstall the app, or immediately via Settings → Reset app data.

Your ad-free purchase. The one-time ad-free upgrade is tied to your Google Play account, not your FridgeChef account. If you delete your FridgeChef account and later make a new one, tap Restore purchase in Settings to get ad-free back. Deleting your account does not entitle you to a refund; refunds are handled by Google Play.
